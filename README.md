# Mochi's Mealtime

A phone-sized browser game about keeping Mochi, a fluffy white British Shorthair, happy from breakfast to bedtime.

Open `index.html` in a phone browser (or any browser) to play. No install, no build tools needed.

## How to play

- Mochi's thought bubble shows what he wants. Tap the matching food in the tray before the ring runs out.
- Serve at the right time. Food he didn't ask for makes him grumpy, and so does waking him from his nap.
- When he says "Notice me…", tap Mochi himself for a cuddle.
- From Day 2 the bubble starts as a clue and the picture appears later. Answer from the clue alone for a Mind Reader bonus.
- The routine card (top right) lists his daily schedule and what each clue means.

| Time | Phase | He wants |
|---|---|---|
| 7–10 AM | Breakfast | Kibble or chicken pâté |
| 10 AM–1 PM | Late morning | Water, cat grass or a cuddle |
| 1–2:30 PM | Nap time | Let him sleep |
| 2:30–6 PM | Afternoon | Treats, water, cat grass or a cuddle |
| 6–9 PM | Dinner | Salmon or chicken pâté |
| 9–10:30 PM | Evening | Treats, water or a cuddle |
| 10:30 PM | Bedtime | Lights out |

## Files

- `src/mochi.html`: the game source (HTML, CSS and JS in one file).
- `index.html`: the playable page, generated from the source by `./build.sh`.
